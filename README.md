## 📅 How to Add to Your Application

### 🌐 Google Calendar (Web & Mobile)
1. Open [Google Calendar](https://calendar.google.com) on a computer browser.
2. In the left sidebar, find **"Other calendars"** (*Andere Kalender*) and click the plus (**`+`**) icon.
3. Select **"From URL"** (*Per URL hinzufügen*).
4. Paste the copied link address and click **"Add calendar"**.
*The layout will update on your web client and sync seamlessly to your phone's Google Calendar app.*

### 📱 iPhone & iPad (Apple Calendar)
1. Open the iOS native **Calendar** app.
2. Tap **"Calendars"** at the bottom center of the screen.
3. Tap **"Add Calendar"** in the bottom-left corner, then choose **"Add Subscription Calendar"**.
4. Paste the copied subscription link and tap **"Subscribe"**.

### 💻 Mac OS (Apple Calendar)
1. Launch the **Calendar** application.
2. Select **File** in the top taskbar menu -> **"New Calendar Subscription..."**
3. Paste the URL link and click **"Subscribe"**.
4. *Tip:* Adjust the "Auto-refresh" dropdown selection to **Every day** or **Every hour**.
